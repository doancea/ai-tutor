# Phase 6 — Integration & Practice

_Part of the [CCAR-F study plan](../claude-learning-resources.md). Cross-domain review — not a scored exam domain itself._

**Estimated hours:** 6–10

## Tasks

- [ ] Review whichever domains scored lowest across Phases 1–5 self-checks (and the diagnostic pre-test, once taken)
- [ ] Take 2+ full practice exams
- [ ] Re-confirm the official CCAR-F exam guide once more before booking

## Practice exam options

| Resource | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [claudecertificationguide.com/mock-exam](https://claudecertificationguide.com/mock-exam) | Free, no sign-up | Full 60-question/120-min mock mirrors the real blueprint; quick 28-question/56-min option also available. Unofficial. |
| Tutorials Dojo CCAR-F practice exams | Paid | Scenario-style, four modes (randomized/timed/review/section), flashcards. Unofficial. |
| OpenExamPrep | Free | Domain-weighted practice blocks. Unofficial. |

All third-party practice resources are **unaffiliated with Anthropic** — treat scores as a readiness gauge, not a guarantee.

## Readiness signal

Anthropic sets the real exam's cut at 720/1000. As a rule of thumb, scoring comfortably above that on **two consecutive full-length practice exams** (not just one good run) is a reasonable bar before booking — scenario-based exams have more score variance than recall-based ones, so one strong result can be noise.
